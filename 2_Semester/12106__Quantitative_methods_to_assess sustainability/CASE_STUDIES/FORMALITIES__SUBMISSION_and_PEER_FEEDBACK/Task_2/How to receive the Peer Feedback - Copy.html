Instructions of how to receive Peer Feedback:

  1. Read Individually: Carefully read all the received peer feedback.
  2. Group Discussion: Present the feedback to your group study members the next time you meet. Discuss if and how you would like to revise your submission based on the feedback.
  3. Critical Evaluation: Critically evaluate the feedback. Decide which points are relevant and useful for revising your submission.
  4. Seek Guidance: Feel free to discuss the relevant feedback with teaching assistants and case study teachers when possible in the teaching sessions of the case study teaching
  5. Submit Revisions: Final version of task 2 is included as an annex in the task 3 submission to enable the teacher to follow any changes that have been made as a result of the peer feedback (see ABOUT THE COURSE - Deadlines).
