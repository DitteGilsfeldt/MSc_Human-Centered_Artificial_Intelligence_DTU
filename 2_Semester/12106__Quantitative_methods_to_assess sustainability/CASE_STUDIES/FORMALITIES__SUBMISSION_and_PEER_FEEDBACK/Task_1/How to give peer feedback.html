Formalities of peer feedback:

Task 1 peer feedback is given in FeedbackFruits-element under CASE STUDIES and Task 1. 

• The peer feedback must be done by each student individually.

• Each student will receive a submission from two other groups within the same case study.

• Each student must give peer feedback to two other group submissions.

• The peer feedback is given based on the criteria described.

• All criteria must be addressed to complete the peer feedback.

• The feedback received is revised by the study group to consider potential changes of the task 1 submission (final version of task 1 is included as an annex in task 2 and task 3 submission to enable the peers and teacher to follow any changes that have been made as a result of the peer feedback).

Use of AI in peer feedback is not allowed.
See deadline for peer feedback under 0.1 About the course and Deadlines.
