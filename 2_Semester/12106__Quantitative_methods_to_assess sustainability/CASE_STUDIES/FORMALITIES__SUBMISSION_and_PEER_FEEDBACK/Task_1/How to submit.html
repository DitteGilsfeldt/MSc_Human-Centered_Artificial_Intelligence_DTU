Formalities of submission:
Task 1 is submitted in FeedbackFruits-element under CASE STUDIES and Task 1. 

• Must cover the Task 1 described in chosen Case study description.

• Must be submitted by only one student from each group.

• Must include a Table of Contributions with an overview of which group members contributed to which parts of the different tasks. The table of contributions must be agreed upon by all group member before attaching it to the submission.

Unless anything else is described in the case study description, the following is applicable:

• Should be a maximum of 7200 characters - corresponding to three normal pages (excluding bibliography and tables).

• All use of language models should be declared.

• No specific format is required.
