Instructions of how to give Peer Feedback:

Focus: Provide feedback on your peer's case study task submission, specifically how it addresses the case study task description.

  1. Course Content: Base your feedback on the course content from both the general modules and the case study module (including video lectures, literature, exercises, and previous case study teachings). If you refer to other content, make specific references.
  2. Avoid Minor Errors: Focus on substantive feedback rather than minor spelling or grammatical errors.

Model for how to give peer feedback:

Use the model for the different aspect of the submission you choose to give feedback on.
