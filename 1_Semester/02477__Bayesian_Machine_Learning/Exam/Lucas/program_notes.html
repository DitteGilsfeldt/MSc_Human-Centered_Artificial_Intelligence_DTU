Bayesian Machine Learning Course Program

Weekly overview of topics, videos, and reading material

Week 1

Program

  • Introduction to Bayesian inference and the course
  • Basic concepts: prior, likelihood, and posterior distributions
  • The Beta-binomial model for estimating proportions
  • Conjugate models and functional forms
  • Simple A/B testing using the Beta-binomial model

Videos

  • Lecture1_part1: Introduction to the course
  • Lecture1_part2: Bayesian Machine Learning in a nutshell
  • Lecture1_part3: Recap of terminology and notation from probability theory
  • Lecture1_part4: The binomial distribution and maximum likelihood
  • Lecture1_part5: Bayesian inference
  • Lecture1_part6: Introduction to exercise sessions
Parts 1 and 2 introduce the course and Bayesian machine learning. Parts 3 and 4 are a warm-up for part 5 and may be faster paced. Part 5 introduces the first Bayesian analysis of the Beta-binomial model.

Reading Material

  • The Beta distribution: Murphy1, Section 2.7.4, pages 63–64
  • Likelihood functions and MLE for Bernoulli: Murphy1, Sections 4.0–4.2.3, pages 107–111
  • Section 4.2.2 is technical and can be skipped
  • Poor man’s Bayes and MAP estimation for Bernoulli: Murphy1, Sections 4.5–4.5.1, pages 120–122
  • Prior, likelihood, posterior, and evidence: Murphy1, Sections 4.6.0–4.6.1, pages 129–130
  • The Beta-binomial model: Murphy1, Sections 4.6.2, 4.6.6, 4.6.7, pages 129–137
  • Credible intervals: Murphy1, Section 4.6.6, pages 146–147
