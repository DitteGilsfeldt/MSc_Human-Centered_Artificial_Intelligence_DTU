DTU Computing Center (DCC) Guides

Here is a list of guides published by DTU Computing Center (DCC), who manages the DTU HPC fascilities. You can find the guides at https://www.hpc.dtu.dk/?page_id=2534 or click one of the links below.

Accessing the LSF 10 Cluster: Basic info on how to log on to the cluster. Similar material as the Connecting to an HPC Terminal guide.

Batch Jobs under LSF 10: Info on job scripts and how to submit jobs, i.e. bsub options and usage.

Managing Jobs under LSF 10: Info on submitting and monitoring jobs. Touches on

  • bsub
  • bstat
  • bkill
  • classstat
  • nodestat
  • showstart,
  • bhist
  • bpeek
  • bacct

LSF Job Templates: Generic batch job scripts for different use cases. Also useful for learning more advanced options for bsub.

Using Modules: Info on the module system. Good info, but not needed for this course.

LSF Queue Parameters: Useful parameters for a subset of the HPC queues.
